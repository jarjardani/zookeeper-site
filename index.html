Zookeeper

A personal, self-hosted pet-care app for one household.

Zookeeper helps one family look after their pets: feeding schedules, medications, vet records, home pet cameras and care instructions for pet sitters. It runs on a computer in the owner's home and is reachable only by that household and the sitters they invite. It is not a public service, and there is no sign-up.

Zookeeper can optionally connect to the owner's own Google account to find vet and insurance emails, send care instructions to a pet sitter from the owner's Gmail, and save files the app creates to the owner's Google Drive.
