Privacy policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Zookeeper is a personal app run by one household on its own home server. It has no public users, no advertising and no analytics. This policy explains what it does with data, including data from Google accounts.

Where data lives

Everything Zookeeper stores (pet records, schedules, camera recordings, and Google access tokens) stays on the owner's home server. Nothing is sent to the developer, sold, or shared with third parties.

Google user data

If the owner connects a Google account, Zookeeper requests only these permissions and uses them only for the purposes below:

  • Gmail read-only: to search the owner's mailbox for messages from their vet, pet insurer and pet-supply sellers, and pull pet-care details (visit records, vaccinations, claims, orders) into the app.
  • Gmail send: to send care instructions to a pet sitter from the owner's own address, only when the owner presses Send.
  • Google Drive (files created by the app only): to save files Zookeeper creates, such as care packets, to the owner's Drive. It cannot see other Drive files.
  • Basic profile and email: to show which account is connected.

Google data is processed on the owner's home server and is not transferred to anyone else. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train AI or machine-learning models. No person other than the owner reads it.

Zookeeper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect Google in Zookeeper's settings, which deletes the stored tokens, or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
